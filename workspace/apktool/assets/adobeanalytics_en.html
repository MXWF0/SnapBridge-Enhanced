Usage Data Collection by Adobe Analytics

This app uses Adobe Analytics, a service provided by Adobe Inc., to collect and analyze usage data, enabling improvements to be made on an ongoing basis.

This app uses identifiers for mobile devices (including iOS advertising identifiers and Android Advertising IDs) and technologies similar to cookies to run these analytical tools.

Data collected by these analytical tools include the following. These data are collected in a form that does not allow identification of individual users.

  1. Your location and actions while using the app
  2. Your browsing activity
  3. Information about your device (including device and browser type, ad ID, operating system, connection speed, and display settings)
  4. Your IP address (or a part of your IP address, depending on the configuration of your solution), which may be used to determine your approximate location
  5. Your mobile device's location information

For more information on how Adobe uses the data gathered by the app, see Adobe’s privacy policy.

Users who have consented to the collection and analysis of data by Adobe Analytics can withdraw their consent and disable the Adobe Analytics feature. To do so, follow the instructions below.

Select “App” > “App options” > “Analyze usage data” and remove the check from “I consent”.
